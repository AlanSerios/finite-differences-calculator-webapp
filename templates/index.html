what are finite differences?

Finite Differences is a simple math trick used to estimate a derivative (slope) when you don't have a calculus formula. Instead of complex calculus, it uses basic arithmetic to find the rate of change between nearby data points.

There are 4 simple steps

1

identify givens

  • x = The target point where you want the slope.
  • h = The step size (distance between data points).
  • method = forward, backward, or central
2

collect data

  • f(x) - Target Point
  • f(x + h) - One Step Ahead
  • f(x - h) - One Step Behind
3

pick your formula

Forward:
$$ \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h} $$
Backward:
$$ \frac{f(x) - f(x-h)}{h} $$
Central:
$$ \frac{f(x+h) - f(x-h)}{2h} $$
4
